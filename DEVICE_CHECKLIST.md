# Physical-device acceptance checklist — pending

Target: Galaxy Tab S10+, Android 10 or newer (minSdk 29). No physical device was
available during this review. None of the checks below is marked as passed.
Back up existing app data before replacing an app signed with a different key.

1. Install the signed APK, launch it, and confirm the editor fits the display.
2. Draw a room, add/move walls, add doors/windows, then undo and redo.
3. Use S Pen pressure, palm rejection, interrupted strokes, and finger input.
4. Place a panel, socket, light, switch, two va-et-vient switches and a contactor.
5. Assign circuits; route cables; build the terminal network and inspect diagnostics.
6. Simulate a lamp, toggle its switch, and check open circuit and short fault behavior.
7. Pair va-et-vient switches and exercise all four position combinations.
8. Assign a contactor control; check its manual/control switch operation.
9. Change 2D/3D mode; rotate, zoom and pan; hide walls and isolate a circuit.
10. Step through Tableau, Saignées, Gaines, Boîtes, Fils, Appareils.
11. Toggle conduits/boxes/conductors and run route animation.
12. Copy/paste and duplicate floors; confirm device associations and circuit isolation.
13. Close/force-stop/relaunch and verify the active project is restored.
14. Export CSV and PDF into Downloads/ELHARIKA; open both in external viewers.
15. Exercise a large project; inspect latency, memory use and long PDF summaries.

Known scope limits: schematic Canvas-based 3D, simplified logical simulation,
one locally persisted active project, and mixed English/French UI labels.
The marketing illustration is not an application screenshot.
